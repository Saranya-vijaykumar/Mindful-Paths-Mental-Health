Patient Agreement

Terms of Care & Clinical Agreement

Clear boundaries and mutual commitments create the emotional safety necessary for deep healing. Please review our outpatient engagement policies.

Outpatient Clinical Agreement · Effective: 2026 Academic & Clinical Year

1 Informed Consent & Therapeutic Process

Psychotherapy is a collaborative endeavor that yields profound personal growth, reduction in anxiety, and relationship renewal. However, the process also involves recalling difficult emotions, memories, and somatic tension. You have the sovereign right to determine the pacing of your sessions, decline specific somatic exercises, and ask questions regarding any clinical methodology.

2 24-Hour Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Your appointment time is dedicated exclusively to you. If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, we require a minimum of 24 hours advance notice via the client portal, email, or telephone. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments are subject to the full session fee, as that dedicated clinical hour cannot be offered to clients on our intake waitlist.

3 Emergency Crisis Disclaimer

Outpatient Status & Emergency Limitations:

Mindful Paths is an outpatient counseling sanctuary and does not operate an acute emergency psychiatric unit. In the event of acute suicidal intent, physical injury, or life-threatening crisis, do not wait for an email reply. Immediately call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or report to the nearest hospital emergency room.

4 Fees, Insurance & Superbills

Our practice is out-of-network with private commercial insurance to maintain the highest standard of unhurried care without insurance company diagnostic caps. Payments are due at the time of session. We provide itemized monthly Superbills with complete diagnostic and CPT billing codes for clients wishing to submit claims to their insurance provider for out-of-network reimbursement.
