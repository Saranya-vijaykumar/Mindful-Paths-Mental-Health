Polyvagal Somatic Grounding Exercises

Physical practices to communicate safety directly to the brainstem via the vagal nerve complex.

1

Physiological Double Sigh

Take two consecutive nasal inhales (one deep, followed immediately by a quick second top-up sip), then release a slow, audible mouth exhale for 6-8 seconds.

Rapid Carbon Dioxide Offloading
2

Bilateral Butterfly Tapping

Cross your hands over your chest, interlocking thumbs at the sternum. Gently alternate tapping your collarbones rhythmically like butterfly wings for 2 minutes.

EMDR Interhemispheric Calming
3

Cold Water Vagal Activation

Apply a cold compress or splash cool water across the temples and upper cheeks for 30 seconds to stimulate the mammalian dive reflex and slow acute tachycardiac heart rate.

Parasympathetic Brake
